Tetris
Hey and welcome to our Tetris made in C#.

To launch the game:
You will need to clone or download this repository.
You must have the NET5.0 framework.
Go to the Folder Tetris and write "dotnet run" in a terminal.

The game:
Once launched, you'll be taken to the main menu. You can then launch the classic Tetris game via the “Start Game” button.

Game controls will be on the left, and other controls on the right.
Here are the main ones:
To turn your Tetrimino: Up arrow.
To go left: Left arrow 
To go right: Right arrow
To accelerate the descent of your Tetrimino: Down arrow.

Mechanisms:
Tetriminos are generated randomly.
You can store your current Tetrimino and see your next Tetrimino.
You can pause the game at any time.
You can save and load your game when you want.

Bonus Features:
You can change your controls at any time in the Option menu.
You have a main menu when you can launch the game and choose between "Play", "Game Mode", "1v1", "Options", and "Quit".
Your screen clears when you reach a score of 3000.
The higher your score is, the faster your Tetriminos will descent.
The best score of all your games is saved and displayed in classic mode only.
In the main menu, a game is played at random.
In 1v1 mode, a malus is assigned to your opponent when you complete a line (The speed of your Tetrimino will be accelerated for 3 seconds.).
A game over menu is available when you lose a game. This asks you to either replay or quit.
Game Mode:
You can play a game mode where you're only allowed to use one Tetrimino and a 1v1 mode on the same computer.
